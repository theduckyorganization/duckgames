# Lucky Duck Shoot

A family-friendly carnival shooting gallery game made with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Add it to GitHub

1. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` to the top level of your repository.
2. Open your repository's **Settings**.
3. Select **Pages** under **Code and automation**.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Select the `main` branch and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.

GitHub will show the website address after publishing finishes.

## How to play

- Press **Start Game**.
- Click or tap the moving wooden ducks.
- Each round lasts 30 seconds and gives the player 10 shots.
- Yellow and blue ducks score 10 points; pink ducks score 20.
- The best score is saved in the player's browser.
